Role of Selling Card 1 of 14

Personal selling

Define it, then say why it matters.

One person talking to one customer, face to face or by phone, to make a sale.

Its strengths are interaction and adaptability; its weakness is cost. A sales visit costs far more than an email.

The General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade, 1947: a provisional set of rules to cut tariffs and quotas, with no institution behind it.

Replaced by the WTO in 1995.

WTO

Define it, then say why it matters.

The World Trade Organization, 1995: a permanent body of 164 members that sets trade rules, covers goods, services and intellectual property, and settles disputes with binding decisions.

Faster and enforceable where GATT was slow and non-binding.

Guanxi

Define it, then say why it matters.

Personal connections in China that a person draws on for resources or advantage in business.

Literally pass the gate and get connected. Built through introductions, favours and gifts; favours are banked and must be returned.
