# Communication & Content Preferences

- Prefers plain, simple explanations over jargon-heavy or formal ones; will explicitly reject a definition as "unclear" or "the worst definition" and ask for something simpler. Confidence: 0.85
- Want explanations anchored to a relatable, concrete example (e.g. using a "gradient from black to white" to explain the word "continuum"), especially one tied to the subject matter being studied. Confidence: 0.8
- When editing study material, keep the source/book's exact terminology rather than replacing it; surface hard words through inline glosses (e.g. hoverable definitions) instead of rewriting the text. Confidence: 0.75
- Expects feedback to be applied to *every* flagged item, not just some — will call out when a requested change (e.g. revising bullet points) was only partially done. Confidence: 0.65
